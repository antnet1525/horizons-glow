Now, what exactly is horizons-glow? Well there's two parts to it.

Before we get to that, let's note it's a github repo name, so apostrophes don't exist. So it could be horizon's glow or horizons glow, kept intentionally vague.

So first, horizon's glow. I think a central theme of a lot of this was the glow of the "horizon," horizon being definitionally "the line at which the earth's surface and the sky appear to meet." But metaphorically, I like to think of it as when we meet, and it becomes bright like the sun is just rising; the brightness of our reunion.
So yeah there, our reunion today, January 6th, is this "glowing" moment that I've been so eagerly looking forward to. Marked by the glowing final gold star, the cherry on top for our tree.

So what about the plural one? Horizons glow? Well yeah, they do. But this reuses the metaphor to go beyond this one reunion.
Everytime I see you, my heart flutters. Every "horizon," every time we meet, whether it be in person, on call, or any definition of the word, my whole world glows. You light up my world, princess, and I am so so grateful for it. I'm genuinely the luckiest guy in existence because of what we share and how lucky I am to have you.
I miss you so much, but in such a good way. I'm so excited to see what our future holds as we navigate it together, and the many more horizons that we'll experience that bring us closer together through their glows.
