Jasmine

Babe,

I know you like poems, and maybe this isn't as high level as you'd like, but I hope you enjoy it and its obvious to nonobvious messages.

Jasmine opens after the world's exhale,
Evermore, what blooms keeps pointing ahead.
A quiet sweetness moves without a name,
Nothing visible, yet all is changed now.

Even the world gently lowers its voice,
Through soft descents, arriving one by one.
Time now learns how to hold its own breath still,
Everything listening, patient, waiting.

Pale warmth lingers briefly upon the lips,
A quiet sweetness held against the cold.
Nothing wasted, dissolving as it meets.

In morning light, the world relearns its shape,
Lines now softened, distances made gentle.
On the horizon, vision learns to rest.
Vast fields resolve into a single truth,
Emerging light holds the whole view still now.

Yet glass encloses a small drifting world,
Only now arrival finds its rest here.
Under a gentle hold, my whole world rests.

Love,
